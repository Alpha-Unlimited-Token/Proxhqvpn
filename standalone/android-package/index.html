Step 1 — Install WireGuard

ProxhqVPN uses WireGuard as its VPN engine. Tap the button below to install it from the Google Play Store.

▶ Install WireGuard on Google Play

WireGuard is free, open source (GPLv2), and made by Jason A. Donenfeld.

Or sideload the APK

If you don't have access to Google Play, you can sideload the WireGuard APK directly:

📦 Download WireGuard APK (F-Droid)

On your phone: Settings → Apps → Install unknown apps → allow your browser

Step 2 — Sign In to ProxhqVPN

Sign in to your ProxhqVPN account. Your personal VPN configs will generate automatically — one for each server.

🔑 Sign In to ProxhqVPN

Opens in your browser. Don't have an account? Sign up free.

After signing in:

  1. 1
    Go to WireGuard Config in the left sidebar
  2. 2
    Tap Generate Config for each server, or download the All Servers Pack
  3. 3
    You'll download a .conf file (or a zip with all configs) — come back here when ready

Step 3 — Import VPN Configs

Import your ProxhqVPN configs into the WireGuard app. You can import all 4 servers at once.

Import from file (.conf or .zip)

  1. 1
    Open the WireGuard app
  2. 2
    Tap the + button (bottom right)
  3. 3
    Choose "Import from file or archive"
  4. 4
    Navigate to your Downloads folder and select proxhqvpn-all-servers.zip (or individual .conf files)
  5. 5
    All 4 server configs appear — tap the toggle next to any server to connect

Or scan QR Code

  1. 1
    On your computer, go to ProxhqVPN → WireGuard Config and click Show QR Code
  2. 2
    In the WireGuard Android app, tap + → Scan from QR code
  3. 3
    Scan the QR — the config imports instantly

✓ You're all set!

ProxhqVPN is ready on your Android device. Here's how to use it:

🔌
Connect: Open WireGuard → toggle the server you want → connected in seconds
🔄
Switch servers: Toggle off the current server → toggle on a different one
🛡
Kill switch: In WireGuard settings, enable "Exclude Private IPs" for split tunnel, or connect to "All traffic" for full tunnel
🌐
Dashboard: While connected, open your browser and go to your ProxhqVPN dashboard for network stats, settings, and more
