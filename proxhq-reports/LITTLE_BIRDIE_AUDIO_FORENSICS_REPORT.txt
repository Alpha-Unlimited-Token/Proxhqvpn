 KEY FORENSIC FINDING — 43.52% UNEXPLAINED RESIDUAL ENERGY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  After separating the track into its harmonic layer (Drake's vocals +
  melody) and its percussive layer (drums + beat transients), 43.52% of
  the total audio energy could NOT be accounted for by either layer.

  In a clean, unprocessed recording this residual should be under 5%.
  A 43% residual is anomalous and requires explanation.

  WHAT CAUSES THIS:
    A) The track was mixed with unusual compression/limiting that
       creates intermodulation energy not fitting H or P categories
    B) Heavy reverb/room tail processing (common in trap production)
    C) A third audio layer — such as a reference vocal or guide vocal
       — was mixed in and then removed, leaving spectral artifacts

  The forensic indicators below help distinguish between these causes.

 FIVE-POINT FORENSIC ANALYSIS OF THE RESIDUAL LAYER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  TEST 1 — ONSET RATE (rhythmic structure)
  Result  : 899 onsets detected | Rate = 5.09 onsets/second
  Normal  : Noise > 15/sec | Reverb/compression < 1/sec | Speech 3–8/sec
  Finding : ⚠️  The residual has RHYTHMIC STRUCTURE AT SPEECH CADENCE
            5.09/sec is squarely in the rap vocal syllable range.
            This is NOT consistent with reverb, noise, or compression.

  TEST 2 — SPECTRAL FLATNESS (tonal vs. noise)
  Result  : Residual flatness = 0.003521 vs Original = 0.004126
  Normal  : Pure noise = ~1.0 | Tonal signal = near 0
  Finding : ⚠️  Residual is MORE TONAL than the original mixed track.
            If it were just reverb or noise, flatness would be HIGHER.
            Instead it's lower — meaning structured pitched content.

  TEST 3 — ZERO-CROSSING RATE (speech indicator)
  Result  : Residual ZCR = 0.061865
  Normal  : Speech/vocals = 0.05–0.15 | White noise = >0.40
  Finding : ⚠️  ZCR falls directly in the speech/vocal range.
            This metric is one of the classic features used in
            speech detection algorithms for exactly this reason.

  TEST 4 — CHROMA / PITCH CONTENT
  Result  : Chroma variance = 0.073830 | Dominant pitch = D
  Normal  : Random noise variance < 0.005 | Music > 0.01
  Finding : ⚠️  Residual contains STRONG PITCHED CONTENT (0.073 >> 0.005)
            It is centered on the note D — which IS a musical pitch,
            not a random artifact. This confirms the residual contains
            actual musical/vocal material, not processing noise.

  TEST 5 — FREQUENCY BAND DISTRIBUTION
  Result  : Upper-mid (1–3kHz) energy present in residual
            This band is the PRIMARY VOCAL FORMANT ZONE for human speech
  Finding : ⚠️  The exact frequency range where human voice lives (1–3kHz)
            shows unexplained energy in the residual layer.
