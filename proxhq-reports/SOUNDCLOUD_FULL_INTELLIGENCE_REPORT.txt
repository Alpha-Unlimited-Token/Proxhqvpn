  recording. Every use, license, broadcast, and streaming payment is
  tracked to this code. It is registered with:

    1. RIAA (Recording Industry Association of America)
    2. IFPI (International Federation of the Phonographic Industry)
    3. UMG's internal rights management system
    4. DistroKid/TuneCore/CD Baby (whoever distributed this to DSPs)
    5. Every DSP: Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon, Deezer, Tidal, etc.

  ─────────────────────────────────────
  The ISRC links to the Sound Recording copyright (SR), which lists
  every contributor. The corresponding ISWC (musical work number)
  links to the composition copyright (PA), which lists every writer
  and producer with their exact percentage splits.

  Step 1: Search copyright.gov for ISRC USUG12604790
           → Will show the SR registration filed by UMG
           → Lists: all performers, producers, engineers, studios

  Step 2: Search ASCAP repertory (ascap.com/repertory) for:
           Title: "Little Birdie" | Artist: Drake
           → Will show the PA registration
           → Lists: writers, publishers, and PRODUCER credit

  Step 3: Search BMI repertoire (repertoire.bmi.com) for same
           → Cross-reference both PROs to get full picture

  Step 4: Subpoena UMG using ISRC USUG12604790 as the identifier
           → They must produce: full split sheet, producer agreement,
             session logs, all contributor agreements
