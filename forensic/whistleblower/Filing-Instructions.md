## Path selection

Before filing, choose your path:

### PATH A — File yourself (no attorney, identity disclosed to agency, fastest)
- **Best for**: people who want speed and don't mind their name being on the filing internally at the SEC/CFTC
- **Time to file**: 1–3 hours total (both agencies)
- **Cost**: $0
- **Anonymity**: Agency knows who you are. Public does not. Operator does not (filings are confidential).
- **Award keep rate**: 100% of any award

### PATH B — File through whistleblower counsel (anonymous, professional drafting, highest award statistical hit rate)
- **Best for**: people who want full anonymity and don't want to handle the paperwork
- **Time to file**: 1–4 weeks total (mostly attorney intake and engagement letter signing)
- **Cost**: $0 out of pocket (contingency: 25–40% of any award)
- **Anonymity**: Full. The agency knows your attorney; the agency does NOT know you. Even internal agency files reference you only by counsel.
- **Award keep rate**: 60–75% of any award after attorney contingency

**Recommendation for this case:** PATH B. The case is strong enough that the attorney cut is more than justified by the higher hit rate, the anonymity, and the professional drafting. Suggested counsel selection criteria are in `Counsel-Engagement-Brief.md`.

The rest of this document covers PATH A. If you choose PATH B, hand the entire `whistleblower/` folder plus the evidence ZIP to your attorney and they will handle filing — you do not need to read further.

## PATH A — Step by step

### Step 1: Set up a dedicated whistleblower email

Do **not** use your primary email. Create a fresh one (Proton Mail is the standard choice — `your-handle@proton.me`). Use only for these filings. This:
- Protects your primary inbox from any agency correspondence that might be observable
- Gives you a clean evidence trail of every reply
- Lets you abandon the address cleanly after the case closes if you want

### Step 2: SEC filing

1. Navigate to **https://www.sec.gov/whistleblower** and click "Submit a Tip" → "Form TCR (Tip, Complaint, or Referral)"
2. Choose the electronic filing option
3. Fill the form. The form is mostly demographic and case-categorization checkboxes. Spend most of your time on:
   - **"Type of complaint"** — select "Fraudulent solicitation of investments" and "Unregistered offering of securities"
   - **"Description of the violation"** — paste a 200-word summary from the top of `SEC-Form-TCR-Narrative.md` here. Mention that the full narrative and evidence are attached.
   - **"Supporting materials"** — attach:
     - `SEC-Form-TCR-Narrative.md` (convert to PDF first using any markdown-to-PDF tool, or just paste as text into a Word doc and save as PDF)
     - `BRD-Market-MASTER-Dossier.zip` (the full evidence package)
4. **Important:** check the box that says you want to be considered for a whistleblower award under Section 21F. If you don't check it, you waive any award rights.
5. Submit. You will receive a confirmation number — save it permanently.

### Step 3: CFTC filing

1. Navigate to **https://www.whistleblower.gov/** and click "Submit a Tip" → "File Form TCR"
2. The CFTC form is structurally very similar to the SEC's
3. Fill it out using `CFTC-Form-TCR-Narrative.md` as your source material
4. **Important:** check the equivalent box for award consideration under CEA § 23 / 7 U.S.C. § 26
5. **Reference your SEC filing** in the CFTC narrative — note that you have filed concurrently with the SEC and provide the SEC confirmation number. This is encouraged and helps the agencies coordinate.
6. Attach the same evidence ZIP
7. Submit. Save the CFTC confirmation number permanently.

### Step 4: (Optional) UK FCA and NCA referral

The operator's confirmed UK landline (Mansfield, +44 1623 302190) provides UK jurisdiction. While neither the FCA nor the NCA operates a whistleblower bounty program, parallel UK pressure increases the SEC/CFTC sanction pool (which IS bountied). Submit:

1. **UK FCA Whistleblowing**: https://www.fca.org.uk/about/promoting-better-culture/whistleblowing — short web form
2. **UK NCA SAR (Suspicious Activity Report)**: only available via regulated entities, but the FCA filing will route this onward as appropriate

### Step 5: Record everything

Maintain a permanent file with:
- Both confirmation numbers (SEC + CFTC)
- The exact date/time of each submission
- A copy of every attachment submitted (hash each one with SHA-256 so you can prove later that nothing was altered)
- All agency correspondence received

### Step 6: Wait, and do nothing

- **Do not** contact the SEC or CFTC to "follow up" unless they reach out first
- **Do not** post about the case on social media (compromises anonymity)
- **Do not** contact "BigTrap" again — any further contact after filing creates risk of obstruction allegations being made AGAINST you
- **Do not** discuss the case in writing with friends/family (creates discoverable communications)

If either agency wants to interview you, they will reach out via the email or phone you provided. Realistic timeline: first contact 30–90 days after filing if they're going to open a formal investigation, or silence indefinitely if they're not. Silence does not mean rejection — it often means the matter is queued behind a larger investigation.

## What to do if BigTrap reaches back out before you file

1. **Don't engage substantively.** Either go silent or send a one-line "I'm thinking about it, give me a few days." Buy time without committing.
2. **Screenshot anything new** and timestamp it.
3. **Add the new screenshots to the evidence ZIP** before filing.
4. **Do not send him any actual money under any circumstance, even a small "test deposit"** — this would compromise your standing as a non-victim and could create a "victim of fraud" narrative that, while still actionable, lowers your standing as a clean whistleblower.

---

## What to do if BigTrap reaches back out AFTER you file

1. **Do not engage further.** The filings are confidential, but anything you say to him after the filing date can become discoverable and complicates the case.
2. **Forward any new contact to your attorney** (if PATH B) or save it to your evidence folder for the agencies (if PATH A).
3. **Block him** on TikTok and any other channel. The filings are done; he's not your problem anymore.
