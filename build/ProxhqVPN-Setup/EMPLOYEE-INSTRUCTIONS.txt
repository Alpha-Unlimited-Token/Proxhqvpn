QUICK START (3 steps)
----------------------
  1. Extract this ZIP file anywhere on your PC (e.g. your Desktop).
  2. Double-click  setup.bat
     → Windows may ask "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"
     → Click YES (required to install WireGuard).
  3. Follow the wizard — click Next, agree to the license, then Install.
     WireGuard and ProxhqVPN will install automatically.

That's it! A ProxhqVPN icon will appear on your Desktop when done.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE INSTALLER DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  ✔ Downloads & installs the official WireGuard VPN client
  ✔ Creates a ProxhqVPN launcher on your Desktop
  ✔ Adds ProxhqVPN to your Start Menu
  ✔ Registers with Windows (visible in Settings > Apps)

No manual WireGuard configuration is needed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST LOGIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click the ProxhqVPN icon on your Desktop.
  2. Sign in with the email address provided by your manager.
  3. If you don't have an account, click "Sign up" — use your
     company email address.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  "Windows protected your PC" warning:
    → Click "More info" then "Run anyway"
    → This is normal for unsigned scripts.

  WireGuard download failed:
    → Check your internet connection and re-run setup.bat
    → Or install WireGuard manually from: https://www.wireguard.com/install/

  Nothing happens after double-click:
    → Right-click setup.bat → "Run as administrator"

--------------------------------------------------------------------
UNINSTALLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Option A: Settings > Apps > Search "ProxhqVPN" > Uninstall
  Option B: Run uninstall.ps1 (in this same folder)

  Note: WireGuard is uninstalled separately via Settings > Apps > WireGuard
