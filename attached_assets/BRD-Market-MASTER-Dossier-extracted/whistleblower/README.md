## How to use

You have two paths:

**PATH A — File yourself, direct (no attorney, no anonymity, fastest)**
- Pros: Free, fast, you get 100% of any award
- Cons: Your identity is disclosed to the agency, no legal cover, awards are smaller for pro-se filers

**PATH B — File through whistleblower counsel (anonymous, highest awards)**
- Pros: Anonymity preserved, professionally drafted, statistically wins larger awards
- Cons: Attorney takes 25–40% of any award, paperwork takes 1–4 weeks before filing

Most serious whistleblowers go PATH B. The bounty math still works strongly in your favor even after the attorney cut.

## Submitting both is allowed and recommended

Filing the SAME conduct as both an SEC Form TCR and a CFTC Form TCR is not double-dipping — it is the standard playbook. Both agencies have overlapping jurisdiction over crypto fraud. Both pay separate awards on the same recovered funds.
